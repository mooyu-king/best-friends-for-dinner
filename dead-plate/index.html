Dead Plate

Serve a busy dining room with Rody while the relationship between waiter and chef starts to cast the restaurant in a different light.

Before your first run

For a first sitting, play through the opening scene without an ending guide. Come back after a natural stopping point to compare what an ordinary exchange seemed to mean with what actually followed.

A practical first attempt

Separate the service task from the story clue. During a busy shift, note which table needs attention before crossing the room. Between shifts, give conversations your full attention rather than reading them as another order.

What to watch on a first run

Treat the dining room as a sequence of unfinished tasks. Before leaving a table, decide what brings you back: another request, a delivery, or a chance to check on the guests. Walking across the room without a destination makes service feel more frantic than it needs to be.

A controlled second attempt

The restaurant works in two registers. A detail can be a practical cue during service and take on an entirely different significance in a conversation afterward. Once the pressure eases, slow down. Notice who describes a problem, whose account you are hearing, and what that person stands to lose.

After the opening scene

A full-endings video is a poor first aid for a missed order. If you need help with service, watch only the relevant early shift and pause before a scene change. If you want to compare story outcomes, finish your first run independently and keep a note of the point where your experience differs.

Questions before playing

What kind of game is this?

Serve a busy dining room with Rody while the relationship between waiter and chef starts to cast the restaurant in a different light.

What should I try first?

Separate the service task from the story clue. During a busy shift, note which table needs attention before crossing the room. Between shifts, give conversations your full attention rather than reading them as another order.

What does the opening screen show?

The opening warning is part of the game itself. Read it before starting a shift, especially if violence or disturbing imagery is something you prefer to avoid.

Can I see the guide without starting the game?

Yes. The player only opens after you select Play. The illustrated notes below it do not require a game download.

Does refreshing restart the player?

Refreshing unloads the current frame. Do not use it as an undo button; use the game's own menus and save options where offered.

Why is there a loading screen after I press Play?

The cover belongs to this page; the game downloads its own resources after launch. Wait for its menu before pressing keys repeatedly.

How can I avoid ending spoilers?

Play before opening the optional video below. Footage may show later scenes or endings.
