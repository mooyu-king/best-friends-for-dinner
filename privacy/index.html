Privacy Policy

How this website and its embedded services work

Website information

This site does not provide accounts or ask you to upload files. Hosting providers may process technical request information, including IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the website.

Traffic analytics

We load a traffic analytics script from data.1back.link and send events to its /api/send endpoint to understand page visits and installation interactions. Requests can include technical browser and network information. We do not intentionally attach game saves or contact message contents to analytics.

Game and video providers

Starting the game connects to s.templeofthejackal.com. Loading a video connects to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed service; opening external links connects to the linked provider. These providers may process data under their own policies. Their storage and game-save behavior are outside this site’s control.

Installation and local storage

Installing the site provides a home-screen shortcut. The site may cache its own pages and assets for faster return visits. External games and videos are not included in offline storage. You can remove site data through browser settings; doing so may also remove locally stored game progress.

Contact and changes

If you email us, your email provider and ours process your address and message so we can respond. Do not send passwords, sensitive personal information or game credentials. For privacy questions contact mooyuking@gmail.com. This policy was updated November 7, 2026.
