WHAT KIND OF GAME IS IT?

A story to read,
at your own pace.

What does “kinetic” mean?

A kinetic visual novel follows a linear narrative. Think of reading an illustrated short story on screen: your main activity is following the dialogue, rather than choosing a route through a branching adventure.

Is it a good fit for you?

Choose this when you want a brief reading session with an unsettling tone. If you are looking for an action game or a long decision-driven campaign, consider that distinction before starting.

Read first, watch later

For a spoiler-light visit, begin with the game and leave the videos and optional horror image until afterward. Use the game’s own menu for available settings; this website’s controls manage the frame, not the story or its saves.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before dinner

Is this the official website?

No. This is an independent game hub. Demonward created the game.

Do I need to download anything?

The embedded version opens in your browser. It requires an internet connection. Desktop downloads are available separately from the creator.

Will it work on my phone?

The website adapts to small screens, but compatibility and touch controls depend on the externally hosted game. Landscape orientation may help. Mobile gameplay has not been verified.

Is the story suitable for everyone?

It includes implied violence, poisoning, bullying and unsettling humor. Review the content note before starting, even though the artwork looks friendly.

Can I play offline after installing?

No. Installation gives you a convenient website shortcut. The external game and videos still require an internet connection.
